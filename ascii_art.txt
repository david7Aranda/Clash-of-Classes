ASCII art for Clash of Classes
Created by Claude (an AI assistant made by Anthropic) for David Aranda, November 2026.
The banners were generated with the FIGlet "small" font. The portraits were drawn by Claude.

Tips:
- In C++, a backslash (\) inside a normal "..." string must be written as \\.
  A raw string R"(...)" keeps every character exactly as shown below.
- To show two portraits side by side, pad each line of the left one to the
  same width (for example 24 characters) before printing the right one.
